QUICK GUIDE

Explore the site

Drag with the left mouse button or one finger to orbit. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Click a numbered annotation to inspect a location. Use the orientation buttons to see the site from above, front, left, right, or behind.

Use the timeline to scrub through construction, or select a stage to animate quickly to that point. Roof cutaway follows the timeline from Barn Decorated, unless you click the cutaway button to override it. Option-click Roof Cutaway to restore automatic mode. The HDR background button changes sky visibility only; environment lighting remains active. Space plays or pauses, ← / → steps 10 frames, Home / End jumps to the start or finish, R resets the camera, and ? opens this guide.
